About Greenleaf Electric LLC

Greenleaf Electric LLC is a family-owned and operated commercial electrical contractor established in 2011 and backed by decades of electrical and engineering experience. We proudly serve clients throughout Texas, providing dependable electrical solutions for K-12 schools, fire stations, municipal facilities, commercial buildings, and other public sector projects.

Who We Are

A trusted partner from planning through closeout.

We partner with owners, general contractors, construction managers, architects, engineers, and facility managers to deliver projects safely, efficiently, and on schedule. From preconstruction and budgeting through construction, commissioning, and final closeout, our team is committed to open communication, proactive coordination, practical problem-solving, and quality workmanship every step of the way.
